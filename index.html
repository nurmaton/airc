Final Bottom line

The Engine Verdict

Feynman + Opus 4.6

Impractical for Multi-Agent Workflows: While Opus possesses incredible reasoning power for literature reviews, connecting Feynman via a Claude Pro OAuth login burns through the platform's message limits instantly. You will hit multi-hour lockouts before completing a single thorough literature review.

Feynman + Gemini 3.1

The Superior Scientific Auditor: By selecting the exact same OAuth route but connecting to google-antigravity instead, you bypass standard subscription lockouts and gain a fresh 1M context per session. This allows the engine to actively utilize local tools—cloning GitHub repos, running Mathematica scripts, and delivering absolute mathematical certainty on proofs.

Feynman + Open Models

Avoid for Strict Academic Work: Models like gpt-oss-120b may be fast and cheap, but they act as "rubber-stampers." They will confidently tell you a proof is correct without executing any underlying math or code to verify it.
