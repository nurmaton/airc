Feynman vs Opus 4.6 vs Gemini 3.1

Which AI tool best serves scientific literature discovery, analysis, and synthesis? A side-by-side comparison for researchers.

F

Feynman

Open-source research agent

A multi-agent CLI tool that reads papers, searches the web, writes drafts, runs experiments, and cites every claim — all locally on your machine. Built on Pi and AlphaXiv.

O

Claude Opus 4.6

Frontier reasoning model

Anthropic's most intelligent LLM with a 1M-token context window, adaptive extended thinking, and deep reasoning — a general-purpose engine you point at research tasks.

G

Gemini 3.1

Multimodal AI + deep research

Google DeepMind's frontier model featuring Deep Think mode for symbolic verification and Deep Research for autonomous multi-source geometric and academic report generation.

Bottom line

When to reach for each

Feynman

Choose when you need a structured research pipeline — systematic lit reviews with consensus mapping, paper-to-code audits, or automated replication experiments with GPU access. Workflow guardrails and citation verification come built in.

Claude Opus 4.6

Choose when you need raw reasoning power and flexibility — proving geometric lemmas, reasoning through reviewer comments, debugging LaTeX / TikZ, or synthesizing hundreds of pages in a single context. No preset workflow; maximum adaptability.

Gemini 3.1

Choose when you want verification & broad discovery — Deep Research generates polished reports from 100+ sources. Deep Think pushes into symbolic verification to stress-test your mathematical lemmas. Best for native multimodal visual analysis.
